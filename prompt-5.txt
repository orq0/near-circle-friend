## 3. README Title and Introduction

The actual `README.md` should begin with a professional presentation similar to:

# Near-Circle-Friend aka Ni-Ce-Friend

**Proposed Facebook Messenger Feature • Meta Global AI Hackathon 2026**

*Discover nearby friends. Reconnect in real life.*

Near-Circle-Friend, also known as Ni-Ce-Friend, is a proximity-based social discovery application demonstrating how Facebook Messenger could help friends reconnect in the real world.

The application brings together an interactive proximity radar, geospatial discovery powered by PostgreSQL/PostGIS, and pedestrian navigation using OSRM and OpenStreetMap.

Users can discover nearby participating friends, explore their proximity through a radar interface, and view actual walking routes with estimated travel times and step counts.

Ni-Ce-Friend is developed as an independent concept prototype for the Meta Global AI Hackathon 2026.
